WELCOME TO THE STUDIO

Some stories are written to be read. Others seem to keep talking long after the final page.

The Man-Child Broadcast explores the memories, people, ideas, and life lessons behind Life Lessons of a Man-Child: A Memoir? These conversations take a fresh look at the book—sometimes funny, sometimes emotional, and occasionally more insightful than the author is comfortable admitting.

Pull up a chair, put on your headphones, and listen in.

Two hosts take a surprisingly thoughtful journey through Life Lessons of a Man-Child: A Memoir?

They explore the book’s forgotten childhood stories, its Gen-X heart, the influence of family, the fear of lost creativity, and the lessons that can follow us long after we believe a chapter has ended.

ABOUT THE BROADCAST

The signal is only beginning.

The Man-Child Broadcast is an evolving collection of conversations inspired by the stories, themes, and wonderfully strange corners of R.C. Howard’s memoir.

Some episodes may explore a single chapter. Others may wander into Gen-X nostalgia, writing, family, fear, creativity, movies, games, cats, or whatever curious doorway the Man-Child opens next.

New transmissions will appear whenever inspiration refuses to leave well enough alone.
