VIDEO EXPLORATION 002

The Lens of Make-Believe

EPISODE 002  •  VIDEO OVERVIEW  •  4:59  •  YOUTUBE

What if the made-up worlds of childhood were never merely an escape from life—but one of the ways we learned how to live it?

The Lens of Make-Believe looks more closely at imagination, creativity, memory, and the stories we carry from childhood into the bewildering business of growing older.

It is a guided visual journey through one of the ideas beating beneath the memoir: make-believe does not always disappear. Sometimes it becomes the lens through which we finally understand ourselves.

VIDEO EXPLORATION 003

The ‘Nothing’ Problem: Why We Forget How to Play

EPISODE 003  •  VIDEO OVERVIEW  •  4:39  •  YOUTUBE

At some point, childhood’s glorious “nothing” became adulthood’s dreaded waste of time. But what did we lose when play stopped being reason enough?

The ‘Nothing’ Problem looks at the strange adult habit of dismissing unstructured play—even though those apparently idle hours once taught us how to imagine, experiment, fail, laugh, and begin again.

Through the Man-Child Lens, this exploration asks whether growing older truly requires us to outgrow play—or whether remembering how to play might help us become more fully ourselves.

VIDEO EXPLORATION 004

Finding God thru Writing

EPISODE 004  •  VIDEO OVERVIEW  •  7:59  •  YOUTUBE

What happens when putting a life on the page becomes more than an act of remembrance?

Finding God thru Writing reflects on the connection between faith and the writing life—how revisiting memories, shaping them into stories, and searching for meaning can become a spiritual journey of its own.

Drawn from Life Lessons of a Man-Child: A Memoir?, it considers how we may begin writing to understand where we have been and discover something larger waiting between the lines.

WHY A LENS?

Some stories ask to be seen again.

A memoir tells you what happened. The Man-Child Lens pauses over the curious details—the themes, symbols, memories, and unanswered questions that keep glowing after the page is turned.

Each video will focus on one doorway into the book and invite readers to look through it from a fresh angle.

“Not every story has to end. Sometimes it is only waiting for us to look again.”
