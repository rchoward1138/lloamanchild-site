THE MAN BEHIND THE MEMOIR

Chris, to my friends.
Staff, to the cats.

R. C. Howard · Author of Life Lessons of a Man-Child: A Memoir?

I was born and raised in Fort Smith, Arkansas, and I've made my home in Northwest Arkansas. By day, I work in corporate project management. At home, I'm a husband and cat dad—which involves a surprising amount of project management, with considerably less cooperation.

I'm a Gen Xer. Old games, '80s movies, Saturday morning cartoons: all still part of the wiring. Growing older hasn't convinced me to give any of that up.

I didn't always think of myself as a reader or a writer. Then this memoir happened. What started as a trip through the past became a way to remember the people I love, revisit things I'd buried, and make something worth sharing.

Writing opened another door. Now there are stories, videos, games, and whatever idea refuses to leave me alone next. That's what this place is for.

If something here makes you laugh, remember somebody, or want to create something of your own, I'm glad you stopped by.
