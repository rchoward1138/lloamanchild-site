THE REAL MANAGEMENT

Nine lives.
Plenty of opinions.

The Kitty Kids are family. They also happen to be excellent reminders that every personality deserves its own story.

Garfield

The gentle orange philosopher. A big presence with a surprisingly delicate meow.

Snowy

The flame-point sweetheart who found his voice—and made sure we heard it.

Punkin

An independent explorer, with head pats strictly on his own schedule.

Daisy

The watchful matriarch. Quiet authority, a stubborn streak, and a talent for noticing.

Camo

The tortie provider and proud mother of the four inside Kitty Kids.

Tom

The social grandpa. Head bumps accepted; conversation enthusiastically supplied.

Loki

A sensitive homebody who chooses affection on his own terms.

Stormy

A gray-striped cuddlebug with a generous heart and very little interest in personal space.

Zorro

A dark-furred gentleman with a social streak and a soft spot for his nightly ritual.
