    Controls, access options & adaptation notes

    Use Tab and Enter, or tap the large buttons. No sound or dragging is required. Five senses can be explored in any order. Every discovery has text. Reduced motion follows your device preference.

    Gamepad: D-pad moves between buttons, A selects; use the button alternatives if your controller isn't supported. You can change the sense shortcuts below. No accessibility setting affects your rewards.

    A puzzle hint can help find the five senses. The optional hidden pawprint rewards an unhinted second look; it never affects your Story Seed.
